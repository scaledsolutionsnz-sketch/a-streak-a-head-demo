Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

A Streak A Head respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

This website has no forms and doesn't ask you for personal information. If you call or email us, we only use the details you give us (such as your name, phone number and the service you're after) to reply to you and look after your booking.

Online bookings

When you book online, your details go to Kitomba, the booking service we use. Kitomba's privacy policy covers the information you give them.

Other services on this website

Our home page shows a Google Map and can play videos from Facebook. Those services may set their own cookies when you use them. Videos only load when you tap play.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you at any time. Call us on 03 218 2787 or pop into the salon at 448B Dee Street, Gladstone, Invercargill.
